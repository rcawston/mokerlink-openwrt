generic: pse-pd: don't enable a PI again while disabling it

With software power control, disabling a PSE PI retries the power
delivery of the PIs waiting for power in its power domain, and the PI
being disabled is still one of them, so it is enabled again right away.
An admin disable of a port with a PD keeps the port powered, and every
disable and every unplug leaks budget in the power domain. This affects
the PSE drivers with the static budget strategy, such as the TPS23881.

Add the fix sent to netdev as a pending patch, after the PSE poll helper
and LED trigger series that it applies on top of.

Tested on a MokerLink POE-10G07110GSM, with two Realtek RTL8239 PSE
controllers: a disabled port stays off, and the allocated budget is back
to 0 after a disable and after an unplug.
