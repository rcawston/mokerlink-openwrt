realtek: add RTL9300 port LED driver

Some RTL930x boards wire LEDs to the switch's LED interface that have no
hardware function and that the vendor firmware switches in software,
such as the PoE status LEDs of the MokerLink POE-10G07110GSM. Add a
driver that takes such LEDs under software control as LED class devices,
so that they can follow the PSE LED triggers, with its binding and the
binding change that allows it as a child of the switch, as pending
patches. Package it as kmod-leds-rtl9300-port.
