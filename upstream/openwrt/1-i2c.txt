realtek: add driver for GPIO I2C buses sharing one SCL line

Some RTL930x switches bit-bang several I2C buses on SoC GPIOs with one
common SCL line and one SDA line per bus, which i2c-gpio cannot
describe. The MokerLink POE-10G07110GSM has its fan controller, PoE
controllers and SFP+ cage on such buses.

Add the driver and its binding as a pending patch, and package it as
kmod-i2c-gpio-shared-scl.
