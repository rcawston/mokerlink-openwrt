realtek: add Realtek RTL8239 PSE driver

The RTL8239 is an 8-channel 802.3af/at/bt PSE controller that some
RTL930x PoE switches, such as the MokerLink POE-10G07110GSM, drive
directly over I2C without a PSE microcontroller. The chip needs Realtek
firmware loaded at power-up; it is not redistributable and has to be
copied from the vendor firmware of the device. The driver loads it as
realtek/rtl8239-bt.bin when it is installed. When the PSE PIs have a
supply with a power budget, the PSE core enforces it, with port
priorities.

Add the driver and its binding as a pending patch, and package it as
kmod-pse-realtek-rtl8239.
