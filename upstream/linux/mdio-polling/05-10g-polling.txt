net: mdio: realtek-rtl9300: program the 10G polling registers

C45 doesn't standardise full duplex, or 1000BASE-T full duplex in the
advertisement and from the link partner, so the polling of a C45 PHY
needs to be told in which register and bit to find them. Three
registers do that, as MMD, register and bit: 10G_POLLING_REG0/9/10_CFG
on the RTL930x, 10GPHY_POLLING_SEL2-4 on the RTL931x. They hold one
setting for the whole switch.

The driver leaves them to the boot loader. Set them from the first PHY
in the table that needs them:

- Realtek's 2.5G PHYs (RTL8221B, RTL8224 and RTL8226) mirror their C22
  registers in MMD 31, at 0xa400 plus twice the register number. The
  polling reads BMCR, CTRL1000 and STAT1000 there.
- Aquantia's AQR113C and AQR813 have duplex in PMA/PMD control 1, and
  the 1000BASE-T bits in two vendor registers of the AN MMD (0xc400 and
  0xe820).

Realtek's 10G PHYs are polled through their resolution register, and
OpenWrt leaves these registers at their defaults for them; so does
this. As with the status register, every PHY like the first sets the
registers again, and one that isn't like it gets a warning.

Daniel Golle's RFC [1] set up these registers for the RTL930x, from the
PHY's vendor.
