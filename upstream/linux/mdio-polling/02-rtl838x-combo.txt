net: mdio: realtek-rtl9300: detect the medium of RTL838x combo ports

On the RTL838x, ports 24-27 can sit on a combo PHY such as the
RTL8214FC, with a copper and a fibre medium. Only with bit 7 of
SMI_GLB_CTRL (PHY_MAN_24_27) set does MAC_LINK_MEDIA_STS report which
medium such a port uses.

Set the bit when port 24 has a PHY, and clear it otherwise. OpenWrt's
MDIO driver does the same: it takes a PHY on port 24 for a combo PHY.
