net: mdio: realtek-rtl9300: set the MAC link source when a PHY attaches

On the RTL930x and RTL931x, a MAC takes its link state either from its
PHY, through the polling, or from its SerDes. Which one is a field of
the MDIO controller:

- RTL930x: SMI_MAC_TYPE_CTRL, with one field for each group of four
  ports for ports 0-23 and one per port for ports 24-27, set to a 1G
  PHY, a multi-gigabit PHY or the SerDes;
- RTL931x: SMI_PHY_ABLTY_GET_SEL, one field per port, set to the PHY or
  the SerDes.

The driver leaves these to the boot loader. As Daniel Golle noted [1],
many boot loaders set them up only when their network is started, for
example to load an image over TFTP, and on a normal boot the MACs then
don't follow their PHYs.

Set every MAC to its SerDes at probe, and to its PHY when the PHY
attaches. On the RTL930x the PHYs of a C45 bus are the multi-gigabit
ones and those of a C22 bus 1G PHYs. That gives the same values as the
table of PHY models in OpenWrt's MDIO driver, for every PHY in it,
without knowing the model.

The field stays when a PHY detaches: the polling of the port is off by
then, and on the RTL930x the field can be shared with other ports. A
switch driver has to set it itself for a link whose state comes from
elsewhere, such as a PHY in an SFP module, which isn't on these buses;
OpenWrt's DSA driver does that.
