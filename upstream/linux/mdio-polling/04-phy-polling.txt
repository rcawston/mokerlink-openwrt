net: mdio: realtek-rtl9300: set up the polling of known PHYs

Two more parts of the polling depend on the PHY model:

- Realtek's 10G PHYs (RTL8261N, RTL8264 and RTL8264B) are polled
  through their resolution register rather than the standard ones. The
  polling does that for a whole bus: POLL_SEL in SMI_GLB_CTRL on the
  RTL930x, PRVTE0_POLL in SMI_GLB_CTRL0 on the RTL931x.
- Realtek's PHYs have proprietary 1G and 2.5G "Lite" modes. The RTL930x
  polls for them on the ports set in SMI_PRVTE_POLLING_CTRL.

The driver leaves these to the boot loader as well. Set them up when a
PHY attaches, from a table of the PHYs used with these switches, which
comes from OpenWrt's MDIO driver. A PHY that isn't in the table keeps
what the boot loader set, as now, with a message.

The first PHY that attaches to a bus chooses its status register, and
every PHY like it sets it again, so a PHY that attaches again restores
it. The PHYs of a bus are expected to be alike, and one that isn't gets
a warning. On the RTL931x, two more bits per bus (PRVTE1_POLL and
FMT_SEL_FRC) are cleared, as OpenWrt does for all the PHYs it knows.

On an earlier RFC for this [1], Russell King asked whether only a small
subset of PHYs is used with these switches, as a table of PHYs in the
MDIO driver doesn't scale otherwise. It is a small subset: Realtek's own
PHYs and, on a few 10G boards, Aquantia's; any other PHY is left as the
boot loader set it up. As Russell suggested there, the setup happens
when the PHY attaches, once its driver is bound.
