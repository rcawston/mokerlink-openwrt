net: mdio: realtek-rtl9300: set up the hardware polling

The MDIO controller of the Realtek Otto switches (RTL838x, RTL839x,
RTL930x, RTL931x) polls the PHYs in hardware and sets the MACs from
them. Since Markus Stockhausen's rework the driver turns the polling of
a port off at probe and on when its PHY attaches. The rest of the
polling setup it leaves to the boot loader, and many boot loaders do
that only when their network is started, for example to load an image
over TFTP [1]. This series sets it up in the driver, as OpenWrt's MDIO
driver does:

1. RTL839x: the global polling enable.
2. RTL838x: medium detection on combo ports.
3. RTL930x, RTL931x: whether a MAC takes its link from its PHY or its
   SerDes, from the bus type.
4. RTL930x, RTL931x: the status register of a bus and Realtek's Lite
   modes, from a table of the PHYs these switches are used with.
5. RTL930x, RTL931x: the 10G polling registers, from the same table.
6. All four: the polling of ports without a PHY, which on the RTL93xx
   also carries a SerDes's link state to its MAC.

Daniel Golle's RFC [1] did 3 and 5 for the RTL930x, from
phydev->supported and the PHY vendor, at PHY probe. Russell King
objected to both, and suggested the attach callback this driver now
has. This series sets the polling up from that callback. It takes the
MAC type from the bus type rather than phydev->supported, and puts the
rest in a short table of PHY models. Patch 4 explains why a table: only
a small subset of PHYs is used with these switches, and any other PHY
is left as the boot loader set it up.

Settings that are shared, those of a bus and the 10G polling registers,
are chosen by the first PHY that attaches and made again by every PHY
like it; one that needs others gets a warning. Nothing is reset when a
PHY detaches.

The register layouts and the table come from OpenWrt's MDIO driver,
mdio-realtek-otto.c, written by Markus Stockhausen, with the RTL8264B
entry by Jonas Jelonek.

Merge path: all patches through net-next. The series changes no binding.

Testing: not built or run yet.
