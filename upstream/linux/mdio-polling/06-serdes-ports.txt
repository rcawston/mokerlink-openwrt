net: mdio: realtek-rtl9300: poll the ports without a PHY

The polling does more than read PHYs: on the RTL93xx it also hands the
link state of a SerDes to its MAC. OpenWrt's DSA driver relies on that
for its SFP ports, whose MACs take their link from the PCS. This driver
turns the polling of all ports off at probe, and on again only for a
port whose PHY attaches, so a port without a PHY is never polled.

Take the ports of the ethernet-ports node without a phy-handle for
ports driven by their SerDes, and turn their polling on at probe.
OpenWrt's MDIO driver does so on all four families. A port with a fixed
link, such as a CPU port, is left out, as the MAC driver forces it.
