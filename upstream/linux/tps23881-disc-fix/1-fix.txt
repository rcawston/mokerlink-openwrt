net: pse-pd: tps23881: report DC disconnections

tps23881_irq_event_handler() reads the fault register into ret, then
stores the result of tps23881_irq_event_over_current() in ret, and
passes ret to tps23881_irq_event_disconnection() as the register value.
ret is 0 by then, so the DC disconnect bits of the register are never
looked at, although the interrupt mask enables their interrupt: a DC
disconnect isn't reported to the PSE core. Over-current faults are not
affected, as they are reported as an over-current and a disconnection.

With software power control, a PI whose PD is unplugged then keeps its
budget and its PD detected, and no disconnection is notified. Its port
isn't disabled either, so detection and classification aren't enabled
again for it, and a PD plugged in later isn't detected: as the commit
this fixes explains, the TPS23881 stops them once it powers a port.

Keep the register value in val for both.

Found in code review, not on hardware; build-tested only, as I don't
have a TPS23881.
