This should go in together with, or after, "net: pse-pd: don't enable
a PI again while disabling it" and "net: pse-pd: make room for a PI
without recursing": reporting DC disconnections makes every unplug
retry the power domain. Without the first, the PSE core enables the
empty port again and leaks its budget; without the second, the retry
can recurse until the stack overflows.
