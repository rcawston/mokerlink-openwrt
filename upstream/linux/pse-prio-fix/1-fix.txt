net: pse-pd: make the static budget priorities take effect

With the static budget strategy, a PI whose request doesn't fit in the
budget of its power domain turns off the PIs of lower priority, a whole
priority level at a time, until it fits. Each is turned off with
pse_disable_pi_pol(), which then retries the power delivery of the PIs
waiting for power in the domain. The PI just turned off is one of them,
and the budget it freed is its own, so it is powered again right away:
the PI of higher priority doesn't get the budget, and the priorities
never take effect.

Only leaving out that retry would leave PIs off for nothing: PIs of
lower priority would be turned off also when that can't make room for
the request, and a whole priority level when fewer PIs are enough.

Instead, turn off PIs of lower priority only when the budget they hold,
with the unclaimed budget, makes room for the request, and then one at
a time, the lowest priority first and, within a priority, the PI that
holds the most, until the request fits. Then retry the power delivery
in the domain, for the PIs turned off whose budget wasn't needed in the
end. Only PIs that are enabled count and are turned off, and the retry
skips the PIs that hold budget, so that the PI being allocated, which
holds its budget but is not enabled yet, is left alone.
pse_disable_pi_pol() no longer retries the power domain.

Sashiko, the netdev AI review bot, pointed out the PIs left off for
nothing, in its review of "net: pse-pd: don't enable a PI again while
disabling it" (the Link below), which this applies on top of.
Build-tested only so far.
