net: pse-pd: fix turning off PIs for budget

With the static budget strategy, making room for a PI by turning off
PIs of lower priority recurses until the stack overflows when the PI is
waiting for power: when its PD is classified, and during every retry of
its power domain. On an admin enable, it powers the PIs turned off again
right away, so the priorities never take effect. Patch 2 fixes that,
making room without retrying the domain. Patch 1 first keeps power
delivery from allocating the budget of a powered PI a second time,
which with patch 2 would turn off PIs of lower priority for nothing.

The series applies on top of "net: pse-pd: don't enable a PI again
while disabling it" (v2), which keeps the retry after turning a PI off
that patch 2 removes:
https://lore.kernel.org/netdev/20261019220835.613-1-ross@r-sc.ca/

Patch 2 is not small for a fix. Turning PIs off without a retry is what
stops the recursion; selecting them first, and giving them their budget
back on failure, keep it from leaving PIs off for nothing. I can split
it further if that helps review.

Build-tested only: neither the recursion nor the fixes are tested on
hardware. Kory, a test on a TPS23881 would be welcome.
