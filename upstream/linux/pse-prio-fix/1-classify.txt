net: pse-pd: don't allocate the budget of a powered PI again

With software power control, power is delivered to an admin enabled PI
when its PD is classified and when its power domain is retried, whether
the PI holds budget already or not. Then
pse_pi_allocate_pw_budget_static_prio() requests its budget a second
time and overwrites pw_allocated_mW, so the first allocation is never
freed.

The TPS23881 can report a classification for a PI that is powered:
disabling a PI enables its detection and classification again, and a
retry of the power domain can power the PI before the classification
comes in. A retry also delivers power to a powered PI when reading its
admin state fails, as pse_pi_is_admin_enable_pending() then takes it
for waiting.

Don't deliver power to a PI that holds budget and is enabled, or whose
state can't be read. A PI that holds budget while it is off, as a power
limit change on a waiting PI or a failed disable leaves it, has that
budget freed and allocated again, instead of a second allocation.

Build-tested only.
