net: pse-pd: make room for a PI without recursing

With the static budget strategy, making room for a PI whose request
doesn't fit can recurse until the stack overflows. The PI turns off the
PIs of lower priority in its power domain, a whole priority level at a
time, with pse_disable_pi_pol(), which then retries the power delivery
of the PIs waiting for power in the domain. The PI is waiting for power
itself when its PD is classified, and during every retry of the domain:
after a disconnection, an admin disable or a priority change. The retry
then powers the PI just turned off again with the budget it freed, if it
comes first in the domain, and allocates the budget of the PI again,
from inside its own allocation, which turns the other PI off again, and
so on. If the PI turned off comes after it, the retry powers the PI
itself, from inside its own allocation, which can then allocate it a
second time. On an admin enable, where the PI isn't waiting yet, the PI
turned off is powered again right away, and the priorities never take
effect.

Make room without retrying the power domain:

- Select the PIs to turn off before turning any off: only the enabled
  PIs of lower priority that hold budget, from the lowest priority and,
  at the same priority, from the last one, until their budget and the
  unclaimed budget make room; then leave out those that aren't needed,
  from the highest priority. If that can't make room, nothing is turned
  off. Budget that such a PI holds while it is off is freed, as it is
  not in use.
- Turn them off and request the budget. If that fails, or enabling the
  PI fails and it can be disabled again, give each PI turned off the
  budget it held back and enable it again, without making room for it
  in turn.
- Notify the PIs that stay off as over budget, once that is known.
- After an admin enable or a classification that made room, retry the
  power domain for the budget left over.

Retry the PIs of a power domain from the highest priority, as
ethtool-netlink.rst documents for the power-up order, so that a PI of
lower priority doesn't take the budget that one of higher priority
waits for, only to be turned off for it again. Events reported
together are still handled one PI at a time, in the order of their
index. This drops on purpose the cut-off that prio_max was meant for,
and which never took effect, as it was reset for every PI: a PI of
lower priority can still take budget that a PI of higher priority can't
use, as that one can turn it off later.

As before, only the PIs of the PSE controller of the PI are turned off
and retried, also when its power domain is shared with another
controller. Only the unclaimed budget of the domain's own supply is
counted: when a supply it is supplied from is short of budget, the
request fails after the PIs are turned off, they get their budget back,
and the priorities don't apply to that shortage.

Sashiko, the netdev AI review bot, pointed out in its review of v1 of
"net: pse-pd: don't enable a PI again while disabling it" (the Link
below) that leaving out the retry alone leaves PIs off for nothing. Not
reproduced on hardware; build-tested only.
