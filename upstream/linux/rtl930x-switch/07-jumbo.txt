net: ethernet: realtek: rtl9300: support jumbo frames

The switch passes frames of up to 12288 bytes. Each port's MAC drops
received frames longer than its limit, so set that limit from the MTU of
the port, with room for two VLAN tags and the FCS, and allow MTUs up to
what fits the 12288 bytes. Whether the MAC counts tag bytes is selected
by a bit whose sense isn't known, so it is left as it is, as OpenWrt
leaves it; if they aren't counted, tagged frames with up to eight bytes
more than the MTU pass.

The CPU port and the DMA engine already take frames of that length:
its ring is sized for frames of the longest length, which span eight
receive buffers.
