net: ethernet: realtek: add an RTL930x switch driver

The Realtek RTL9301, RTL9302B, RTL9302C and RTL9303 are MIPS SoCs built
around an Ethernet switch with up to 28 ports. The switch is one
register block, described as a syscon whose children are the MDIO, I2C
and reset controllers, and the CPU reaches it through a DMA engine of
the switch on its port 28, with the source and destination ports in
the DMA descriptors rather than in the frames.

That makes it a switchdev driver with its own DMA, like the Microchip
sparx5 and lan966x drivers, rather than a DSA switch behind a conduit.
The binding already describes it that way.

Start the driver: bind the switch node, take the register map of the
syscon and report which chip it is. The ports, the DMA engine and the
switch setup follow in the next patches.

There is no public datasheet. The register layout and the order of the
setup follow the OpenWrt realtek target, by Birger Koblitz, Markus
Stockhausen and other contributors.
