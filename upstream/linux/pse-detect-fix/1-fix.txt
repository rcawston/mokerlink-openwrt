net: pse-pd: don't drop a classification reported with a detection

pse_set_config_isr() returns right away when the notifications have
PSE_BUDGET_EVAL_STRAT_DYNAMIC set. That is a budget evaluation
strategy, not an event, and its value is that of
ETHTOOL_C33_PSE_EVENT_DETECTION. The TPS23881 reports the detection
and the classification of a PI in one notification when both are
latched by the time it handles its interrupt, and the classification
is then dropped: with software power control, the PD isn't powered.

pse_set_config_isr() is only called for the power domains with
software power control, which pse_pw_d_is_sw_pw_control() never
reports for the dynamic strategy, so the check has nothing to do.
Remove it.

This was found by Sashiko, the netdev AI review bot, in its review of an
unrelated pse-pd fix (the Link below), not on hardware. Build-tested
only: I don't have a TPS23881, and the driver of the Realtek RTL8239
PSE controllers I use reports a detection on its own.
